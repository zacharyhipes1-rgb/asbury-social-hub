### Why these and not others

Each was checked against the brief's rule that imagery must match the training actually
programmed, and against the campaign copy, which names "barbells, kettlebells, rowers and
bodyweight movements". All four of those appear on the page: barbell in the class photo and the
closing photo, kettlebells in the hero and the detail, a rower in the second detail, and
bodyweight work in the band.

The band exists for a specific reason. Between the class section and the closing photo the page
ran roughly 4,500px of unbroken text on a dark background, which is a lot of black to scroll
through. The band also does editorial work: it is the only photo showing a range of ages, so it
is the one that answers "would people like me be in this room".

Two licensed candidates were **rejected after looking at them** and are not used anywhere:

- **486501842**, a coach with a senior client: a bright white studio and very light dumbbells.
  It reads as a light-aerobics class, which is not this program.
- **587552648**, a warehouse gym interior: near empty and almost black, with one set of
  parallettes. It shows a room rather than training, and a dark empty frame is the opposite
  of what this page needed.

Both were licensed before they could be viewed, because every stock preview CDN is blocked
from this build environment and licensing is the only way to see the full image. They are
paid for and available if a use ever comes up.

## Shot list for real photography

Replace all six. Shoot during a real class, get written consent from everyone recognisable.

1. **Hero, landscape.** Mid-class on the floor, a coach in frame talking to an athlete.
   Room for the headline on the left, so keep the subject right of centre. Shoot wide.
2. **Class floor, landscape.** The room working, several people, different ages and builds.
   This is the photo that answers "would I fit in here", so cast it honestly.
3. **Two square details.** Hands on a kettlebell, and someone on a rower. Close, textural.
   These are the two slots the stock currently fills, so match the framing: tight and dark.
4. **Closing, landscape.** Two people finishing a set together. The current stock photo is two
   lifters side by side, which is the "bring a friend" idea in one image. Keep that idea.
5. **Photo band, very wide.** Roughly 3:1. A row of people working through the same movement,
   deliberately mixed in age and build. This is the "would I fit in here" photo, so cast it
   honestly rather than picking the fittest members.
6. **Coaches.** Head and shoulders of each coach named on the page.

## Walkthrough video

The campaign document specifies this one directly. It does not exist and nothing was
generated to stand in for it, because a generated or stock clip would misrepresent the gym.

Sequence to film, roughly 45 to 75 seconds:
parking → front door → where you put your things → filling in the waiver → walking into
class → the coaches introducing themselves.

The player is already built in the page: native controls, `preload="none"`, `playsinline`,
and it respects reduced motion. Until the footage exists the slot renders as a compact
one line note rather than a large empty 16:9 rectangle, so the page does not look unfinished.

To publish: add a poster frame and an MP4 source, remove the `hidden` attribute from the
`<video>`, add `has-video` to the wrapper's class (`class="video-wrap has-video"`, which
restores the 16:9 frame), and delete the `.vicon` icon and `.video-todo` block.
