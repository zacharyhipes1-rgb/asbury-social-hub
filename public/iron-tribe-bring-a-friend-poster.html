Straight answers

Before they ask.

Does my friend need to be in shape first?
No, and that reflex is exactly what the guest week exists to kill. Every movement has a scaled version. The coach gives them theirs before the clock starts, so they work at their level in the same room as everyone else.
Is someone going to hard-sell them at the end?
No. They get a goal-setting conversation before day one and a check-in at the end of the week. If it isn't a fit, that's a fine answer and the week still cost them nothing.
What should they bring?
Training shoes, a water bottle, and about ten minutes of buffer on their first day so the coach can walk them around before class.
Can I bring more than one friend?
Yes. Submit the form once per guest so each person gets their own pass and their own spot in class. Spots are capped, so earlier in the month is easier.
What if they've trained at Iron Tribe before?
The free week is for first-time guests. If they're a former member, send the form anyway and the gym will sort out a return offer instead.
Do I get anything for bringing someone?
Member rewards for Bring a Friend Month are set by each location. Ask your coach what yours is running, or note it on the form and we'll confirm when we reply.
